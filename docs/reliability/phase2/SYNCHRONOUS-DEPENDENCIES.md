## Separate operations and absent dependencies

Finalize (`public.finalize_production_match`) repeats runtime/actor/match/idempotency/permission checks, verifies complete coherent 18-hole progress, revokes access, and creates a bounded immutable scorecard snapshot/archive job atomically. Its capture helper is explicit; legacy capture trigger is disabled. Lock/Resume/Open remain separate canonical operations using the match/admission serialization. They must race safely with score submission.

**PROVEN (POSTGRESQL):** deferral is restricted to hole writes and the match update following a hole write in the same transaction. All hole AFTER triggers finish before the score RPC updates its match; the COMPETITION intent provides an exact `(tournament,match,transaction,family)` routing witness. Standalone match/round lifecycle operations retain original side-game enqueue and shared engine-marker work. `P2-MIG-LIFECYCLE-SCOPE` in [migration-safety evidence](evidence/migration-safety.json) observes zero synchronous enqueue calls for a real score RPC, while actual Lock and Finalize retain their prior calls; a round status trigger retains Calcutta enqueue. This is not complete Open/Prepare/pairing workflow certification.

No direct Odds calculation/publication, leaderboard rebuild, external Google call, notification, SMS or email occurs in the explicit canonical hole RPC. This does not imply optional application work is absent: PWA/native `after()` fans out to Google/archive, competition/intelligence and Calcutta processors. Their failures occur after commit. The Google-authority compatibility path is a different authority mode and is not redefined here.

## Index and row-scope evidence

Existing required keys: `hole_scores(match_id,hole_number)`, `score_mutations(match_id,mutation_key)`, `match_participants(match_id,team_side,player_slot)`, unique `(match_id,player_id)`, `scoring_snapshots(snapshot_id)`. Match progress is limited by the 18-hole key domain. New intent ready-work and unresolved-scope partial indexes prevent processed history from being rediscovered during score write/claim/close. Their write overhead and plans require the candidate benchmark; no speculative score-table indexes are added.

The additional `google_writer_fence_rehearsals_unrestored_idx` is justified by measured restored-history scans. Predicate: `status='RUNNING' OR (status='FAILED' AND NOT restoration_confirmed)`. It indexes `run_id` and preserves the original guard truth table. The new intent table has four indexes including its primary/unique keys; the rehearsal guard adds one index to an existing table.
