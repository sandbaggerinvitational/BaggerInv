# Director milestones and exit gates

1. **D-OPS1 P0:** current health, GO, status and receipt read model. Depends on typed backend errors and exact operation status. Exit: the owner can distinguish committed, not committed and unknown outcomes, and see readiness blockers from an iPhone.
2. **D-OPS2 P0:** self-certifying Prepare, Open, Lock, Resume, Finalize and closeout. Depends on atomic RPC contracts. Exit: lost response, concurrent change and rollback all yield the correct visible result.
3. **D-OPS3 P0:** Physical Scorecard Recovery. Depends on canonical score admission and same-ID status. Exit: the owner recovers 18 holes across matching, missing, conflict and concurrent-fill cases without SQL or Codex.
4. **D-OPS4 P1:** side-game lifecycle, exact dependencies, supersession review, ownership correction and Next Safe Action. Exit: the post-R2/R3 and financial-correction rehearsal completes safely.
5. **D-OPS5 P1:** Tournament Close, archive, recent operations and brief. Exit: all golf, financial decisions and the historical snapshot are verified.

Migration risk: the receipt schema and new views must be additive and versioned. Rollback retains original canonical mutations and receipts; never delete new competitive records to restore an old UI. Every milestone includes mobile owner acceptance and wrong-role security proof. Director tournament readiness means all normal operations complete without Terminal, SQL, log interpretation or engineering.
