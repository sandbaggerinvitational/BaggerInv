# Evidence gaps and collection plan

## Missing or incomplete evidence

- exact physical request → server trace correlation;
- phone-local native queue persistence and transitions;
- native tab and shell recreation reasons on affected physical sessions;
- provider I/O budget, IOPS, throughput, I/O wait, and connection-pool time series;
- provider restart reason;
- complete older certification-claim language;
- exact owner intervention durations;
- retained query plans from before the incident;
- current backup retention and a proved restore test;
- formal final side-game, champion/archive, and Close Tournament receipts.

These gaps limit causal closure and frequency estimates. They prevent a complete capacity claim, attribution of each physical return to Today, and a claim that all historical integrity was audited. Evidence gaps are first-class requirements, not permission to guess.

## Collect for 2027

Collect privacy-neutral stable IDs and request, operation, and mutation correlation; domain and phase error categories; latency and commit/readback status; release, activation, and contract versions; worker state and input revision; provider resources and restarts; and native intent transition, network class, and shell-recreation reason.

Do not place names, email addresses, phone numbers, tokens, or raw gross-score payloads in metric labels. A protected audit may contain the minimum necessary competitive facts under access control.

Retain one-minute resource history, event history, and request traces long enough to cover the pre-event period through postmortem. Choose exact retention against provider limits and cost; do not assume it is currently available. Keep release and operation evidence immutable for the approved long term. Tier or downsample raw high-volume telemetry. Mirror critical provider alerts into an access-controlled incident archive. Temporary directories are not sufficient as the sole authority.
