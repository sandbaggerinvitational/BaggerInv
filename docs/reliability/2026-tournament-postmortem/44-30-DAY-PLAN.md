# First 30 days and first week

## First week

Day 1: review the incident register, evidence gaps, proposed priorities and scope; approve a durable retention destination.

Day 2: sanitize and retain the evidence manifest; map critical function and contract ownership and test IDs.

Day 3: instrument the isolated score and health trace chain and design the provider metric collector; separate diagnostic credentials and targets.

Day 4: add deterministic SQL claim, queue date and historical score-cost regressions; capture baseline plans and fixed seeds.

Day 5: review ADRs for the score transaction, feature/session separation and semantic dependencies; demonstrate one timeout diagnosis and one unknown-outcome trace.

Do not begin a broad rewrite before these are understood.

## Weeks 2–4

Week 2: complete the real SQL/persistence baseline and resource telemetry; implement the bounded score-path seam and outbox in isolation, with negative security tests and a rollback plan.

Week 3: prototype current-pointer and semantic dependencies, define the job status/operation receipt contract, and begin Build 11 queue/navigation source work after contract review; execute measured history-growth tests.

Week 4: integrate the first Director GO/status views and physical-card review design, run a non-live restore/admission drill, and publish an evidence-backed progress review.

The 30-day target is reproducible incident fixtures, observable critical paths, a measured performance baseline, an enforced diagnostic boundary, approved contracts, and the first bounded fixes with integration proof. This is not a promise that Build 11 or tournament certification will finish within 30 days. UI polish and new features wait.

Acceptance: the OBS-CORE-001, PERF-001 and CERT-NA-001 foundation; agreed BE-SCORE-002 and B11 contracts; retained telemetry without secrets; real SQL execution and baseline plans; and the first restore/admission evidence. Missing capacity metrics remain explicit blockers.
