## Ten highest-value changes

1. Bound the canonical score transaction and enforce historical-growth and plan budgets for 2026-INC-019. Acceptance: NA-2026-019 proves exact RPC correctness and latency under accumulated history, and scoring remains safe when the derived worker is unavailable.
2. Make persistence/reconciliation and feature/session isolation the Build 11 core for 2026-INC-006 through 2026-INC-009. Acceptance: NA-2026-006 through NA-2026-009 cover physical, long-lived, lifecycle, and failure matrices at their specified proof layers.
3. Make critical operations self-certifying with exact receipts and status for 2026-INC-013, 2026-INC-017, and 2026-INC-018. Acceptance: an unknown response resolves without creating a new operation.
4. Establish proof-layer and freeze gates plus stable Never Again chronology for all escape classes. Acceptance: missing physical or SQL P0 evidence blocks readiness.
5. Narrow dependency inputs and expose jobs and lifecycle for 2026-INC-011, 2026-INC-016, 2026-INC-017, and 2026-INC-019. Acceptance: post-R2 to R3 flows complete without hidden engineering interpretation.
6. Implement Director physical-card recovery for 2026-INC-020. Acceptance: the owner safely handles missing, matching, conflicting, and concurrent states.
7. Instrument score, domain, resource, and navigation correlation for 2026-INC-025. Acceptance: an injected failure can be identified from retained traces and metrics without sensitive payloads.
8. Enforce diagnostic isolation plus capacity and restore gates for 2026-INC-022 through 2026-INC-024. Acceptance: heavy primary reads are denied or redirected, and soak and restore rehearsals pass.
9. Certify PWA as an independent client path with safe handoff for 2026-INC-014 and 2026-INC-006. Acceptance: hosted configuration and physical multi-hole handoff pass without duplicating an unknown intent.
10. Preserve durable evidence and current history, and make runbooks owner-centered. Acceptance: the owner completes the rehearsal without routine engineering and the evidence remains reproducible after temporary files are removed.

Ranks reflect qualitative risk reduction, not measured dollars or hours. Each work package has acceptance and confidence in the requirements and backlog. Cosmetic expansion and provider migration do not outrank scoring reliability.
