# Automation policy and implementation order

**Audit boundary:** historical evidence and source review only. No Production/staging requests, mutations, builds, deployments or source changes in this audit. Proposed designs are not implemented or approved release evidence. Unknown measurements remain unknown.

## Authority policy

Fully automate observations, exact canonical readback, current cache invalidation, evidence generation, invariant checks and bounded derived projections. Safely supersede obsolete unpublished jobs only after proof that no publication or running claim depends on them. Every automatic mutation needs a certified operation class, exact input version, stable idempotency key, audit receipt and bounded retry policy.

Owner confirms Open, Lock, Resume, Finalize, pairings, handicap approval, public side-game publication, financial changes, physical-card commit and tournament close. Preparation can be executed automatically only as the already-approved exact preparation operation; changing participants or authority remains a separate owner decision. Do not infer authority from a green health flag.

Never automatically resolve different Official gross values, invent physical scores, change approved handicaps, alter pairings after play, repair historical competitive facts, change ownership fractions or ignore safety guards. Automatic protective admission suspension for genuinely unavailable/invalid authority is distinct from changing round lifecycle with Lock.

## Order and safety tests

1. Correlation/metrics plus small current-state snapshot.
2. Exact operation receipts/status and post-mutation readback.
3. Round GO and closeout checks.
4. Job visibility/stale detection, then proven safe supersession.
5. Side-game phase advisor and Next Safe Action.
6. Protected physical-card recovery.
7. Certification orchestration, impact selection and claim generation; foundational test infrastructure starts in parallel from day one.

Each automation must pass duplicate execution, stale authority, wrong trigger, concurrency, cancellation, lost response, worker crash, partial failure, receipt reconstruction and permission denial. On uncertainty it reports the unresolved state instead of mutating authority to make a dashboard green. A review screen expiring invalidates authorization to changed facts.

[Machine-readable catalog](automation-catalog.json) and [master table](52-AUTOMATION-MASTER-LIST.md) specify trigger, reads, writes, approval, failure and receipt for every proposed automation.
