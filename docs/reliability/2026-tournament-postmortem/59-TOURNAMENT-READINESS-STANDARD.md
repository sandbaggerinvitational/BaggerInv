# Formal Tournament Ready standard


TOURNAMENT READY is a conjunction of fresh evidence, not a percentage. Every P0 domain must PASS: competitive authority, native/PWA/Director scoring, round controls and R3 sequencing, side games, database/capacity, security, fallback/recovery, physical device, full lifecycle, observability and release control. P1 requirements that remain operational blockers are also mandatory; accepted limitations must be genuinely noncritical.

Required: frozen exact native/backend/schema/contracts; full chronological synthetic tournament; physical multi-user all-format scoring; owner rehearsal without routine engineering; SQL/RPC paths; performance/history growth/capacity; failure injection; unknown-outcome recovery; side-game lifecycle; physical-card/restore fallback; staged compatibility; bounded hosted readback; zero unresolved P0 defects and zero missing P0 proof; reviewed P1 limitations with workaround and expiry. Never turn NOT PROVEN into FAIL or PASS: it is a separate release-blocking uncertainty for critical capabilities.

Engineering complete means source/tests/contracts/build are finished with no known P0 defect. It does not mean tournament certified. Build11 shipped is not tournament ready. Current 2026 system should not be trusted as primary 2027 software without these gates.

Hard blockers are machine-enforced: critical test missing/failing/flaky; absent physical scoring; failed chronology/R3; unbounded critical SQL; performance/headroom violation; unknown schema compatibility; fallback/unknown outcome/security/data-integrity failure. Exact thresholds are adopted only after measured baseline and rehearsal; do not manufacture green from unspecified limits.

Certification scorecard domains use PASS/FAIL/NOT PROVEN, evidence, last verified, physical proof, limitations and blockers. No hidden weighted aggregate. Evidence ledger schema and change graph drive expiration.
