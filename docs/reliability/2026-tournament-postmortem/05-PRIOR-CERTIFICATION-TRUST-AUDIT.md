## Certification trust judgment

**STRONGLY SUPPORTED:** overall confidence was too high relative to missing proof dimensions. **PROVEN:** particular wrong-layer tests could not catch observed defects. **UNKNOWN:** the complete wording and history of every earlier freeze claim and every owner acceptance gesture; surviving reports are incomplete. No exhaustive historical statement inventory is claimed.

Future vocabulary: SOURCE INSPECTED; UNIT PASS; SQL/RPC PASS; API PASS; SIMULATOR PASS; PHYSICAL IPHONE PASS; STAGED/HOSTED PASS; PRODUCTION READBACK VERIFIED; FULL-LIFECYCLE PASS. Always attach scope, build, fixture, time, artifact, and limitations. Use NOT PROVEN for absent evidence, FAIL for an observed failed requirement, and PASS only within the exact layer. Owner acceptance lists interactions; it is never a universal endorsement.
