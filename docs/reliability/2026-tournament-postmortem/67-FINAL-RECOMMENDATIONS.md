# Final recommendations in requested order

For readable executive interpretation, see [00-EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md](00-EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md) and [OWNER-DECISION-PACKAGE.md](OWNER-DECISION-PACKAGE.md). Technical evidence and stable requirements are linked from [INDEX.md](INDEX.md).

## 1. WHAT FAILED

Repeated failures affected the native queue and navigation, SQL, PWA configuration, activation-bound jobs, dependency and preparation workflows, whole-round Open, score latency, and database availability. The incident register contains 26 stable entries. They are not all independent outages, and one physical symptom can have multiple contributing causes.

## 2. WHY IT FAILED

Independent immediate defects combined with coupled state, synchronous derived work, and missing current operational models. The Sep 27 score timeout has a proven query-path cause. The exact cause of the Sep 26 resource outages remains UNKNOWN.

## 3. WHY WE MISSED IT

Testing used the wrong layer, fixture, lifecycle, sequence, or volume for several failures. Physical and chronological proof was inadequate, capacity evidence was missing, and some broad PASS interpretations exceeded the original reports' stated limits.

## 4. WHAT WORKED

Canonical server authority, gross-only input, atomic rollback, idempotency, fail-closed guards, receipts and readback, the repaired PWA, physical cards, and careful reconciliation preserved the examined competitive golf.

## 5. WHAT MUST CHANGE

Bound critical paths, isolate failure domains, narrow semantic dependencies, preserve valid native context, and productize supported recovery.

## 6. WHAT SHOULD BE AUTOMATED

Automate bounded readback, status, GO, closeout, stale-state detection, health, correlation, and evidence capture. The exact automation catalog defines write and approval boundaries.

## 7. WHAT SHOULD REMAIN MANUAL

Keep competitive and financial judgment, public financial publication, conflict resolution, pairing and handicap changes, final Close, and emergency release approval under explicit owner authority.

## 8. BUILD 11 PLAN

Limit Build 11 to reliability scope rather than a full visual or feature rewrite. The isolated SQLite serialization correction can begin without waiting for the broader backend redesign. Integrate navigation, typed errors, and coordinated read contracts only after their actual dependencies are stable. Treat Build 10 relaunch as bounded, non-guaranteed recovery; it is not a permanent fix or physical certification.

## 9. BACKEND PLAN

Add a minimal transactional event seam, bounded current pointers, exact operation status, and semantic job contracts. Move nonessential historical and derived work off the score transaction.

## 10. DIRECTOR PLAN

Provide mobile health, Next Safe Action, GO, atomic controls, exact receipts, physical recovery, and closeout.

## 11. DATABASE PLAN

Execute real SQL entrypoints, retain index and plan evidence, enforce a historical-growth budget, bound connection and lock scope, and retain audit evidence.

## 12. INFRASTRUCTURE PLAN

Harden Supabase and Vercel first. Measure headroom, isolate workloads, verify backup restoration, and rehearse failure. Evaluate an AWS proof of concept only if measured requirements remain unmet.

## 13. TESTING PLAN

Run the stable Never Again tests, exact SQL/API contracts, performance budgets, failure injection, full synthetic chronology, physical devices, and owner rehearsal. Do not promote a simulator or component PASS to physical or full-lifecycle proof.

## 14. 2027 DRESS REHEARSAL

Run the complete chronology, including post-R2 to R3 transition, side games, unknown outcomes, native/PWA handoff, fallback, closeout, and archive evidence.

## 15. FIRST FIVE ACTIONS

The authoritative list is in [77-FIRST-FIVE-ACTIONS.md](77-FIRST-FIVE-ACTIONS.md): evidence preservation → observability → realistic baselines → approved contracts → diagnostic and workload isolation.

## 16. 30-DAY PLAN

Establish the evidence and measurement foundation and begin bounded, exact fixes. This phase does not establish Tournament Ready status.

## 17. 90-DAY PLAN

Integrate backend, native, Director, and capacity controls; run the synthetic, physical, and owner programs; then rerun final freeze proof close to the 2027 tournament. The schedule is a planning sequence, not a guarantee.

## 18. FINAL P0/P1/P2/P3

P0 items are strict readiness gates. P1 items provide required robustness. Cosmetic changes and feature expansion remain deferred unless separately approved.

## 19. REMAINING RISKS

Provider, network, device, human, and new-defect risks remain. Each requires explicit mitigation, detection, and fallback. Unknowns remain UNKNOWN until evidence closes them.

## 20. FINAL VERDICT

The 2026 operational experience was unacceptable. The examined canonical golf was preserved within the retained evidence. Build 11 and broader reliability work are required. This audit performed no fixes to application source or Production.
