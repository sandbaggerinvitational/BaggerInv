# Measurable 2027 outcome


Owner: normal tournament completed through mobile Director with zero SQL/Terminal/Codex interventions; every round has GO/closeout receipt and clear next action; financial decisions remain owner-reviewed. Emergency signing may remain an explicitly accepted exceptional engineering action.

Player: gross entry commits once, confirms and advances; no unexplained shell reset; network loss preserves intent; fallback explains pending/Official distinction. Spectator: bounded fresh-enough public views; no private money-game/identity leakage; optional delayed reads do not starve scoring.

Engineering: all known2026 failure classes have regression evidence, current contracts are versioned, no P0 missing proof, every critical change has impact-selected tests and rollback. Operations: measured capacity/headroom, current health dashboard, noise-controlled actionable alerts, tested restore and physical-card recovery.

Targets: zero lost committed Official scores; zero duplicate commits from same ID; zero unauthorized competitive changes; zero uncontained feature→session resets;100% critical mutations reconcile to receipt/readback or visible unresolved status;100% round GO/closeout; zero known unbounded history queries on scoring path; zero red-health round opens; zero routine engineering interventions. Latency/availability objectives are proposed in performance/SLO docs and must be calibrated, not invented as achieved.

Residual risk remains: provider outage, damaged phone, human transcription mistake, unknown software defects and communications failure. Success means containment, reliable fallback and recoverable authoritative outcomes—not a guarantee of zero bugs.
